Perfection BY RIRI - Ads reporting

App privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy covers the private Ads reporting tool operated by Perfection by Riri in Lynnwood, Washington. It describes the Google Ads connection used by the salon owner and the salon’s advertising specialist. It does not replace the salon’s policies for appointment bookings or payments.

Data accessed

After an authorized operator grants Google access, the tool can retrieve information from the salon’s authorized Google Ads account. The reporting and planning workflow uses account identifiers, campaign and ad settings, keywords, available search terms, advertising costs, performance and conversion reports, and keyword-planning estimates.

Google authorization credentials and tokens support the connection. Operators authorize through Google; they do not enter their Google account passwords into the Ads reporting tool. Search-term reports may contain text entered by search users.

How data is used

The salon uses this information to understand its advertising performance, research keywords, diagnose measurement settings and prepare advertising recommendations. Supported advertising changes can be submitted through the separate management connection after an authorized person confirms the exact proposed changes.

Data obtained through this connection is used for the salon’s advertising workflow. The salon does not sell this data or use it to provide advertising services for unrelated businesses.

Who processes and can access data

Access is limited to the salon owner and the salon’s advertising specialist. The local MCP server communicates with Google Ads. Data returned to Codex is processed by OpenAI to provide the requested analysis and assistance; selected information can therefore be included in chat history, tool outputs and generated reports.

Google and OpenAI process information under their applicable service terms and privacy policies. Service-side storage and retention depend on the account settings and terms of those services. This workflow is not limited to processing on the operator’s computer.

The salon’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Storage and retention

Google authorization credentials are handled by the locally configured Google authentication libraries. Advertising data may be retained in operator-created reports, local tool logs and Codex chat history.

The salon owner and advertising specialist keep saved advertising reports while they are needed for the salon’s advertising work and delete them when they are no longer needed. Chat history and service-side copies are managed separately through the relevant service’s controls and retention policies. Deleting a local report does not automatically delete those copies.

Revocation and deletion requests

An authorized Google user can revoke the app’s access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access through that grant but does not automatically delete existing reports or chat history.

To request deletion of salon-controlled saved reports or ask about the connection, contact perfection.byriri@gmail.com. Copies controlled by Google or OpenAI are subject to those services’ deletion controls and retention policies.

Updates and contact

Material changes to the tool’s data practices will be reflected on this page. Contact Perfection by Riri at perfection.byriri@gmail.com with privacy questions.
