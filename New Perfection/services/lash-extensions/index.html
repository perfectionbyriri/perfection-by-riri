Before your visit

A few things
to know.

Which style should I choose?

Start with how subtle or full you want your lashes to look. You don’t need to know the technical names. Bring a reference or talk through your preferences with Riri.

How many lash extensions will I get?

Every set begins with a minimum of 150 individual extensions per eye, applied one lash at a time for a full, natural-looking finish. The exact number depends on your natural lash density and the style you choose.

How long is the appointment?

Appointment length depends on the set you choose. Riri will confirm timing when you book.

How should I prepare?

Arrive with clean lashes and share any sensitivities with Riri. Any further preparation guidance will be shared before your appointment.
