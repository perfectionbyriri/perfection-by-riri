Perfection BY RIRI - Ads Agent

App privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy covers Perfection BY RIRI - Ads Agent, a private advertising tool operated by Perfection by Riri in Lynnwood, Washington. The salon owner and the salon’s advertising specialist use it to work with the salon’s authorized Meta advertising accounts, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It does not replace the salon’s policies for appointment bookings or payments.

Data accessed

After an authorized operator grants access through Meta, the tool can retrieve account and business identifiers and names; campaign, ad set and ad settings; budgets, delivery status and advertising performance; and change-history information made available by Meta.

Depending on the permissions granted and the requested task, the workflow also accesses authorized Facebook Page and Instagram account identifiers, names and profile information; owned posts, captions, images and videos; and Pixel or dataset configuration and aggregated conversion-event statistics. Operators may provide advertising images, video, copy and destination links for preparing ads. The current workflow does not download lead-form submissions or customer lists.

Meta authorization credentials and access tokens support the connection. Operators authorize through Meta or configure an access token; they do not provide their Facebook or Instagram account passwords to the Ads Agent.

How data is used

The salon uses this information to review advertising performance, understand booking-related events, diagnose advertising settings, prepare audiences and ad materials, and build or review campaign drafts. Supported changes are performed within the operator’s authorized scope; publishing or activating advertising requires the operator’s approval.

Media and copy may be sent to Meta while preparing assets and drafts, before any advertising is published. Creating a draft does not mean the ad has been launched.

The salon uses the connected data for its own advertising workflow. It does not sell this data or use this tool to provide advertising services for unrelated businesses.

Who processes and can access data

Operator access is limited to the salon owner and the salon’s advertising specialist. The configured MCP connection communicates with Meta’s Ads MCP server and advertising APIs. Information returned to Codex is processed by OpenAI to provide the requested assistance and may appear in chat history, tool outputs and generated reports.

Meta and OpenAI process information under their applicable terms, privacy policies and account settings. The workflow is not limited to processing on the operator’s computer. Credentials are configured separately from the public website and are not included in this policy or published reports.

Storage and retention

Advertising information may be retained in operator-created reports, private project documentation, local tool logs and Codex chat history. The salon retains saved advertising reports while they are needed for its advertising work and deletes salon-controlled copies when they are no longer needed.

Authorization credentials are stored using the configured local environment, authentication storage or service settings. Chat history and service-controlled copies are managed separately through Meta’s and OpenAI’s controls and retention policies. Deleting a local report does not automatically delete those copies.

Revoking access

An authorized operator can revoke the application’s permissions through Meta’s Business Integrations or relevant business settings. A business administrator can also remove a system user’s assigned assets or revoke its access token, where that authentication method is used.

Revocation stops future access through the revoked authorization. It does not automatically remove previously created reports, chat history or advertising objects already stored by Meta.

Deletion requests

To request deletion of salon-controlled information saved by this tool, email perfection.byriri@gmail.com with the subject “Ads Agent data deletion”. Identify the connected business, Page, Instagram account or advertising account and the information you want removed. Do not send passwords, access tokens or payment-card details.

The salon will verify that the requester is authorized and arrange deletion of applicable salon-controlled reports or saved records. Requests concerning copies controlled by Meta or OpenAI are subject to those services’ deletion controls and policies. Deleting a report does not delete an advertising account, campaign or booking.

Updates and contact

Material changes to the tool’s data practices will be reflected on this page. For privacy or deletion questions, contact Perfection by Riri at perfection.byriri@gmail.com.
