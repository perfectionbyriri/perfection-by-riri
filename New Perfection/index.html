ABOUT RIRI

Beauty is personal.
So is my approach.

My name is Marharyta. My path into beauty started with something simple: a fascination with how small, precise details could change the way a person carries themselves. That curiosity turned into a career and, over the past ten-plus years, into real mastery of permanent makeup and lash artistry.

I work with European techniques, which focus on soft, natural-looking results and careful, precise execution. I keep refining my craft through continued training and have taken part in several professional beauty competitions, where my work was judged alongside other artists.

What hasn’t changed is the belief that started it all: beauty isn’t about becoming someone else, it’s about being seen more clearly as yourself. That’s the experience I try to create for every client who trusts me with her face. I work with clients in English, Russian and Ukrainian.
